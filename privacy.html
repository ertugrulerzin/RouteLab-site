RouteLab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

RouteLab (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a network engineering toolkit for Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac. This policy explains how the App accesses, uses, stores and shares information.

1. Information RouteLab handles

RouteLab does not require a RouteLab account. Information you enter, including target hosts, recent queries, remote-server profiles, tool settings and custom lists, is normally processed and stored locally on your device. Credentials, private keys and cloud OAuth tokens are stored using the platform’s protected credential storage where supported.

If licensing is enabled, the App contacts the RouteLab licensing service with a product identifier, license key and device-specific identifier or public key to activate and validate the license. The corresponding private device key is not sent to the service.

2. Google Drive data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect an account, RouteLab requests permission to view and manage all files in that account. This broad permission is necessary because File Explorer lets you navigate the complete folder hierarchy and open, download, upload, create, copy, rename and delete files selected by you. A narrower per-file permission would not provide the complete dual-pane file-manager functionality.

RouteLab receives your Google account identifier, email address and display name to label connected accounts. This metadata and OAuth credentials are stored locally on your device. RouteLab uses Google Drive data only for File Explorer features visible to you. It does not use Google Drive data for advertising, analytics, credit decisions, surveillance, profiling or sale.

Google Drive API data is processed directly by the App on your device. RouteLab does not upload Drive file contents or OAuth credentials to a RouteLab-operated server. When you explicitly start a copy, upload or download, a selected file may be transferred directly between Google Drive and local storage or another destination you connected, including another Google Drive account, Dropbox, Box, SFTP, SMB or FTP. RouteLab does not initiate these transfers automatically.

RouteLab’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Retention and your controls

Cloud credentials remain in protected local storage until you remove the account, clear the App’s data or uninstall the mobile App. Desktop operating systems may retain application data after the App is removed; you can delete RouteLab’s application-data folder or credential-store entries.

Removing a cloud account in RouteLab deletes the OAuth credentials saved by RouteLab for that account. You can also revoke RouteLab access from your Google Account connections. Files copied to another destination remain there until you delete them from that destination.

4. Network requests you initiate

The App sends requests to destinations you specify when using tools such as ping, scan, trace, DNS, HTTP, SSH, FTP, SMB or SFTP. It may also contact third-party network information services, public DNS resolvers, routing and registration databases, geolocation services, GitHub for update checks, platform app stores, and cloud providers you connect. These destinations can observe your IP address and request data and operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Local network and device permissions

Some tools interact with devices on your local network. Wi-Fi scanning and Drive Test can request nearby-device or location permissions where required by the operating system. RouteLab does not request contacts or microphone access for its core functions. Local files are accessed only when you use File Explorer or select a file for an operation.

Connection Location History is an optional iOS feature that you must turn on. While enabled, RouteLab uses Location Services only when one or more SSH or Telnet sessions are open. It records the location where each terminal session was opened, displays those records to you in the App, and may keep the active terminal available while you switch apps. Coordinates and connection-location history are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to RouteLab. You can disable the feature or clear its history at any time. If you explicitly open a saved location in Maps, Apple Maps handles that request under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

6. No advertising or behavioural tracking

The App does not contain advertising or third-party behavioural-tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information or Google user data and do not share it with data brokers.

7. Security

Network traffic uses the protocol selected by you; some protocols, including FTP and unencrypted HTTP, do not provide transport encryption. OAuth exchanges and supported cloud APIs use HTTPS. Protect your device and remote credentials and use encrypted protocols where available.

8. Children

The App is intended for network professionals, students and enthusiasts. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be described by updating the “Last updated” date in the App and on this page. Where required, we will request consent before using Google user data for a new purpose.
